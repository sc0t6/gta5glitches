                11 · Story Mode Money

                Lester's stock market trick

                The best legit way to get billions in Story Mode. Hold off on Lester's assassination missions until after The Big Score, then put every character's money into these stocks.

                MissionInvest beforeInvest afterTypical gain
                1. Finish The Big Score first. It pays out tens of millions to each character.
                2. Before each mission, put all of Michael's, Franklin's and Trevor's money into the "before" stock.
                3. Finish the mission, quick-save, and sleep or skip days until the stock peaks. Sell, then buy the "after" stock and wait for it to peak.
                4. Make a separate save before each mission so you can reload if you miss a peak. Done well, each character ends up with about $2B.

                14 · Ban Safety

                How not to get wiped

                🧾 Space out big sales

                Rockstar flags sudden jumps in money. Don't sell several expensive duped cars back to back, and keep it to a few a day.

                🧪 Use a second account

                Test new glitches on a throwaway character or account first, and keep your main clean.

                🚫 No mod menus or "money drops"

                Paid recovery services and modded lobbies are the #1 cause of bans. Anti-cheat on PC catches injectors fast.

                🔒 Invite-only sessions

                Do glitches in private sessions so nobody can report or record you.

                📉 Spend it, don't hoard it

                Money spent on properties and cars is less likely to be wiped than a giant bank balance.

                🕹 Mix in legit runs

                An account that also runs heists and contracts looks normal. Pure glitch income doesn't.
